STARCLUSTER

A pure C++ data-driven space simulation game. Explore a procedural galaxy of over 10,000 stars, navigate real-time economic markets, and witness slower-than-light diplomacy.

Explore Source Code

Deep Mechanics

Slower-Than-Light

No instant travel. No omniscient maps. Information travels at the speed of light. Local knowledge is stale, signaled, or debug-only.

Dynamic Economy

Every star features a local market with supply, demand, and localized price pressure. Trade, mine, and exploit scarcity.

Procedural Elements

Resources aren't hardcoded names; they are generated properties based on atomic mass, chemical traits, and nuclear stability.

Faction AI

Factions manage budgets, declare colonization missions, calculate risk, and navigate relations in a complex dense matrix.

Data-Oriented

Built in C++ targeting pure performance. Systems use numeric IDs and contiguous vectors, escaping object-oriented overhead.

Signal System

Signal packets propagate through 3D space tracking owner knowledge, market snapshots, and military intelligence.

Physics & Flight

Realistic Newtonian Flight

Acceleration depends entirely on ship mass, thrust, and drive efficiency. Cargo mass changes dynamic performance based on the specific element's atomic weight.

There are no global waypoints—agents evaluate contracts, route risks, fuel cost, and stale intel to make jumps through the interstellar medium.
